VR Adventure Action
VR Combat UX FTUX (Onboarding Design) UI Design Systems Interaction Design (VR) Level Prototyping

Worked on UX design across the full development cycle of an action-adventure VR game, from prototyping to launch. Captain Toonhead vs The Punks from Outer Space
VR Tower Defense
Game UI/UX Systems Player Feedback Design Unreal Engine (UMG)

Designed and implemented interfaces in Unreal Engine for a VR tower defense game, applying a design system focused on improving player decision-making in Survival Mode. Translated complex game states (waves, sector danger, and spatial differentiation) into clear, actionable feedback, reducing cognitive load during real-time gameplay. Additionally, prototyped visual solutions and developed the global scoring system for Arcade Mode.

Killer Klowns From Outer Space: The Game
Asymmetrical Horror
User Research Playtesting Player Behavior Insights

Conducted playtesting sessions as a User Research Intern for an asymmetrical horror game, recruiting external players to evaluate new mechanics and cooperative gameplay flows. Analyzed player behavior and team dynamics to assess clarity of objectives and win conditions across both sides. Insights from these sessions informed key design decisions, including reducing the original large map into multiple smaller maps to improve pacing, navigation, and overall player experience.

ABOUT ME
// DESIGNER · CREATOR · EXPLORER

Game UX Designer
shaping player experiences

I'm a Colombian designer with 3.5 years of experience, focused on UX for video games. My work centers on shaping player experiences that feel clear, responsive, and engaging, with a strong emphasis on how systems communicate with players in real time.

What draws me to games is their ability to speak to people and create coherent, immersive experiences where players feel truly part of the world. I design with that in mind — building systems that translate complexity into intuitive interactions, reducing friction while maintaining experiences that are engaging, challenging, and rewarding.

I believe game experiences are built across multiple layers, where systems, interactions, and spaces come together to guide player behavior. What I enjoy most is designing those systems and environments that create meaningful moments for players — crafting spaces that feel intentional, readable, and alive through the way players interact with them.
