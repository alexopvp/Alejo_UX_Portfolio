// USER RESEARCH INTERN

Killer Klowns from Outer Space is an asymmetrical PvP horror game inspired by the 1988 cult film, where three klowns hunt down seven human players. While the klowns focus on tracking and capturing their targets, humans must work together to escape or find ways to fight back.

During playtesting, I focused on identifying where the experience lost clarity — when players felt unsure about their objectives, or when pacing slowed down and tension dropped. These observations helped surface friction points in how players understood goals and navigated matches, contributing to improvements in pacing and overall experience readability.

01
Players need constant short-term goals
When players didn't know what to do next, engagement dropped and the experience felt slower than intended.
02
Pacing is driven by clarity, not just mechanics
It wasn't only the length of matches affecting tension, but how clearly players understood their role within them.
03
Asymmetry requires balanced understanding, not just roles
Even if roles are different, both sides need equally clear objectives to sustain engagement and fair play.
