Orcs Must Die! By the Blade is a VR action-adventure adaptation of the Orcs Must Die! franchise, originally known for its tower defense gameplay on PC.

In this version, combat shifts from strategic placement to direct, physical interaction — requiring players to engage enemies through movement, timing, and spatial awareness.

As a UX Designer, I defined the combat language — a system of signifiers, feedback, and mechanics that makes combat readable, responsive, and grounded in the player's physical actions.

COMBAT LANGUAGE
DESIGN PROCESS

In VR, the body is the controller — every attack, block, and parry
must feel earned, readable, and physically honest.

WHAT WAS THE CHALLENGE?

Transitioning from a strategy-driven experience to a physically driven one introduced a new challenge: how to make combat immediately readable while players are fully engaged with their bodies in motion.

The system needed to communicate impact, damage, and defensive opportunities in real time — without slowing down the intensity of the experience.
