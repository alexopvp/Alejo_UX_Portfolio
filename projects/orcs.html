What was I working on?
// UX DESIGNER

Orcs Must Die! By the Blade is a VR action-adventure adaptation of the Orcs Must Die! franchise, originally known for its tower defense gameplay on PC.

In this version, combat shifts from strategic placement to direct, physical interaction — requiring players to engage enemies through movement, timing, and spatial awareness.

As a UX Designer, I defined the combat language — a system of signifiers, feedback, and mechanics that makes combat readable, responsive, and grounded in the player's physical actions.

WHAT WAS THE CHALLENGE?

How do you make combat readable when players are fully engaged with their bodies in motion?

Transitioning from a strategy-driven experience to a physically driven one required redefining how combat is communicated.

The system needed to convey impact, damage, and defensive opportunities in real time — without overwhelming the player or breaking the pace of combat.

PLAYER FEEDBACK
Taking Damage
Feedback was designed to clearly communicate when and from where the player was hit, without breaking immersion.
Directional cues and screen effects helped players quickly understand incoming threats, while a critical state intensified the visual feedback — shifting the player's vision and increasing tension during combat.
Dealing Damage
Outgoing damage needed to feel immediate and rewarding. Early iterations relied on enemy color shifts and health indicators, but this was expanded through VFX to differentiate damage types more clearly.
Combined with haptic feedback, these cues reinforced the physicality of combat, allowing players to feel the impact of their actions.
DAMAGE TYPES

Each damage type was designed to create a distinct combat behavior, balancing speed, impact, risk, and distance.

Slashing Damage
Designed for speed and flow, slashing attacks allow players to hit multiple enemies in a single motion.
This type reinforces fast, continuous combat, making it effective for crowd control and maintaining momentum.
Bludgeoning Damage
Focused on impact and weight, bludgeoning attacks deliver strong, deliberate hits that affect a single target.
These attacks generate knockback and are especially effective against armored enemies, rewarding precision over speed.
Piercing Damage
Piercing attacks emphasize precision and physical engagement, allowing players to stab enemies directly.
While riskier due to close-range interaction, they deal higher damage and strengthen the sense of physical presence in combat.
Ranged Damage
Ranged weapons provide distance and control, allowing players to deal damage without direct engagement.
This type adds strategic flexibility, enabling players to respond to threats across space using crossbows and firearms.
BLOCK & PARRY
Block
Block provides a reliable way to mitigate incoming damage, allowing players to defend by physically placing their weapon or shield in the path of an attack.
A white flash on impact signals a successful block, reinforcing clarity without interrupting the flow of combat.
Parry
Parry transforms defense into skill expression, requiring players to not only intercept the attack, but respond with the correct timing, force, and direction.
When executed correctly, it creates a moment of control within combat, opening a window for a powerful counter.
Glory Kill
Successfully parrying an enemy enables a context-based finishing move.
Each weapon unlocks a specific gesture that, when performed correctly, eliminates the enemy instantly — reinforcing mastery through physical interaction.

LEARNINGS
LEARNING 01
// SIGNAL VS NOISE
More information doesn't
mean more clarity
Overloading players with feedback and signals can reduce awareness instead of improving it. Prioritizing what matters helps players focus and act without hesitation.
LEARNING 02
// VR CONSISTENCY
Immersion depends
on consistency
In VR, every visual and physical cue must feel connected to the player's actions. When feedback, mechanics, and movement align, the experience becomes more intuitive and believable.
LEARNING 03
// PLAYER AGENCY
Meaningful choices
drive engagement
Giving players multiple ways to approach combat — through timing, positioning, or weapon type — creates a deeper and more engaging experience, as long as each option is clearly communicated.
