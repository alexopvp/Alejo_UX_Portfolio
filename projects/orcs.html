MY ROLE

A VR action-adventure title within the Orcs Must Die franchise, reimagined with a strong focus on melee combat rather than traditional tower defense. As the UX Designer for the project, I led the design of core interaction systems, including object handling, inventory, weapon upgrades, and armory flows, as well as the full FTUX. I also developed the combat language—defining clear visual and systemic signifiers to guide players on when to attack, block, or parry—ensuring combat felt intuitive, responsive, and satisfying within VR.
